# Features

Signature Studio makes HTML email signatures that look right in Outlook. You add your details, pick a design, adjust the style, check it and copy it into your email app. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

## The editor

The Studio has five sections, in the order you usually need them:

| Section | What it holds                                                     |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Details | Profile, Contact, Photo & logo, and Extras                        |
| Design  | The gallery of designs and styles, Compare, and your saved styles |
| Style   | Layout & colour, Typography, Icons, and Reply                     |
| Check   | The signature health report                                       |
| Use     | Copy, export, install, signature files and Start over             |

You can open any section at any time. The arrow keys move between sections and tabs.

- **Header:** select the Signature Studio logo to go back to the start: Details, with the New message preview. Your work stays. The Start over menu sits beside the logo. On the right are Email profile, App appearance, Open signature file and Save signature file.
- **Email profile:** Outlook, selected by default. It covers new Outlook, Outlook on the web, classic Outlook for Windows, Outlook for Mac and Outlook mobile. Other email apps are not supported yet.
- **App appearance:** Auto, Light or Dark. Auto follows your device. It changes only the editor, never the signature.
- **Preview toolbar:** New message or Reply; Preview or Compare; Light or Dark; Desktop, Tablet or Phone; and Replay for animations. These change only how you view the signature.
- **Under the preview:** an accessibility summary, and Copy signature with the design name, image count and size.
- **Footer:** the app version, build number and build date in UTC. Select it to open the matching GitHub Actions run. Local builds show **Local build** instead.
- **Wide screens:** the header, section tabs, preview toolbar and Copy signature stay in view while the settings and the preview scroll.
- **Phones and narrow windows:** one scrolling column with sticky section tabs, and a bottom bar with Edit, Preview and Copy signature.

## Details

Each field has a show switch beside it. A hidden field is left out of every design and of the plain text.

- **Profile:** Full name, Initials, Name in another language, Pronouns, Job title, Team, Company, Region and Location. A name in another language, such as Arabic, reads in the right direction.
- **Contact:** Mobile, Office phone, Email, Website, Booking link, Working hours and Languages.
- **Links:** LinkedIn, GitHub, Handle, X, YouTube, Microsoft Learn, Blog, and Chat on Teams (built from your email).
- **Extras:**
  - Message and sign-off: Tagline, Banner and Banner link (new messages only), Sign-off and Signed as, and Disclaimer.
  - Optimise images (smaller emails), on by default.
  - Developer designs: the Agent's question and the Code Editor's suggestion.
  - Featured content (below).

## Photo and logo

**Photo**

- Upload or drag in your own photo, or use the default sample photo. Zoom and move it inside its shape.
- **15 shapes:** Auto, Circle, Rounded, Squircle, Square, Arch, Leaf, Slice, Slices, Layers, Hexagon, Octagon, Blob, Polaroid and Stamp.
- **4 sizes:** S, M, L and XL, in every column layout. Replies keep a small photo.
- **4 looks:** Natural, Black & white, Duotone and Sepia.
- **Animation:** Auto, Off, Spin, Sweep, Orbit, Pulse, Comet, Dash, Sparkle, Shine and Draw.
- **Motion:** animations play once and stop within 5 seconds. Turn on **Keep animations looping** to loop them.

Minimal has no photo. Monogram Card shows your initials instead, and the size setting resizes them.

**Company logo**

- Upload a PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP logo, then turn on **Show logo**. No logo is built in.
- It shows beside your company name in 22 designs: Aurora, Fluent Tiles, Leadership, Foundry Mesh, Azure Essentials, Split Card, Handwritten, Executive, Minimal, Highlights and all 12 industry designs.

**The Design section**

- **Show:** filter the gallery by collection (All designs, Featured, Styles, My styles), by role or by industry. Thumbnails show your own details.
- **Styles:** 18 built-in styles. A style is a design plus its settings: colours, columns, photo, icons, name and background.
- **Save as my style** saves the current look, never your details or images. Rename and delete your styles, or **Export** and **Import** them as a `signature-styles.yaml` file.
- **Shuffle a new mix** picks a random, readable look.
- **Compare:** pick designs or styles on their cards and compare them side by side, or compare all.

## Style

### Layout and colour

- **Columns:** Auto (the design's own), 1, 2 or 3. Two columns put who you are on the left and how to reach you on the right. Three columns add a column of link icons.
- **Photo in 2 or 3 columns:** Own column (the default) or Beside name.
- **Contacts one per line**, instead of a grid.
- **Colour theme:** 33 palettes in five groups: Microsoft and tech, Classic and calm, Nature, Warm, and Rich.
- **Colours:** a palette fills in 1 to 4 colours and the **Gradient** switch. Change any of them and the theme shows **Custom**. Each colour opens a picker with suggested colours, your logo's colours and any colour you choose.
- **Colours from a logo or image:** suggests main colours from your logo or another image.
- **Background:** Transparent (the default), or a solid card in the palette's tint, White, Cloud, Sand, Midnight, Graphite or a custom colour.

Text colours adjust automatically to stay readable on light and dark backgrounds.

### Typography

- **Font:** Design default, Arial, Verdana, Trebuchet, Georgia, Times or Courier. Each uses fonts already on the reader's device, with a fallback. No web fonts.
- **Detail text size:** Standard (11 px), Comfortable (12 px) or Large (13 px).
- **Name:** typed, or handwritten. A handwritten name is drawn stroke by stroke and plays once.
- **Handwriting:** 10 styles and 3 pens (Brush pen, Fountain pen and Fine liner). A style that can't write a letter of your name is greyed out.

### Icons

- **Family:** Classic, Rounded, Geometric or Hand-drawn.
- **Style:** 17 styles: Template's own, Text labels, Glyphs, Outline, Gradient, Brand, Rings, Frames, Soft circles, Soft squares, Glass, Circles, Tiles, Rounded, Squircles, Stickers and Depth.
- **Weight:** Style's own, Line, Bold, Solid or Duotone.
- **Colour:** Style's own, Theme, Colourful, Brand links, Two-tone or Grey.

Text labels uses words instead of icons and is the most accessible choice.

### Reply

| Version                | Shows                                              |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| One line (the default) | Name, title, company, mobile and email on one line |
| Two lines              | Name and title, then company and contacts          |
| Colour bar             | Two lines beside a thin colour bar                 |
| Mini card              | Two lines beside a small photo                     |
| Name only              | Name, title and company                            |
| Design's own           | The design's own reply layout                      |

## Check

The Check section tests both the new-message and reply signatures, in light and dark mode, and updates as you edit. Each version has 19 checks, marked **Passes**, **To review**, **Manual check** or **Not applicable**. They cover:

- language and direction, alt text, link names and table roles;
- text size and contrast in light and dark mode;
- supported HTML, images, sizes and sharpness;
- animation length and the still frame;
- handwriting coverage;
- fit on a 343 px phone and survival of an Outlook paste;
- email size and image hosting;
- which of your features show in the chosen design.

**Feature coverage** lists what shows and what doesn't. **Signature requirements** and **Sources** show the Outlook guidance behind the checks. A manual checklist reminds you to paste, send, reply and forward a test in each Outlook app. Passing checks don't certify every Outlook version.

## Use

- **Copy signature** and **Copy reply signature**, ready to paste.
- **Plain text**, for Outlook mobile.
- **HTML code**, **Save .html** and **Classic Outlook (.zip)**.
- **Image hosting:** Embedded (the default) or Hosted on my site, with an image folder URL and **Download images**.

### Install in Outlook

1. Select **Copy signature**.
2. Paste it into Outlook:
   - **New Outlook and Outlook on the web:** Settings > Accounts > Signatures > New signature.
   - **Classic Outlook for Windows:** File > Options > Mail > Signatures. Or extract **Classic Outlook (.zip)** into `%APPDATA%\Microsoft\Signatures`.
   - **Outlook for Mac:** Settings > Signatures.
3. Select **Copy reply signature** and save it as your reply signature.

## Signature files

**Save signature file** writes everything to one `.signature.yaml` file: your details, email profile, style and uploaded images. **Open signature file** loads it again, on any computer or browser.

```yaml
# Signature Studio signature file.
signature: 2
emailProfile: outlook
details: { fullName: Sam Taylor, title: Principal Engineer }
contact: { email: sam.taylor@contoso.com }
extras: { tagline: Building things people love to use }
show: { photo: true, logo: false }
style: { template: aurora, colors: ['#0F6CBD', '#7160E8'], gradient: true, cols: auto }
images:
  photo: { type: image/jpeg, data: <base64> }
  logo: { src: '' }
```

- Every field is written, so the file also works as a template you can edit.
- **Include my uploaded photo** is on by default. Turn it off to save the file without your photo.
- Only embedded images are opened. A web address is ignored, so a file can't make the Studio load anything.
- Keep the file private. It holds your details.

## Start over

Each action asks first.

| Action              | Details and images | Design and settings | Saved styles |
| ------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------- | ------------ |
| New blank signature | Cleared            | Back to defaults    | Kept         |
| Try a random sample | A made-up person   | Back to defaults    | Kept         |
| Reset everything    | A made-up person   | Back to defaults    | Deleted      |

A random sample takes its first name and surname from the same culture, so they always match. Reset everything also resets the Studio's own settings.

## Privacy and accessibility

- The Studio is one HTML file. It works offline and from disk, and makes no network requests.
- Your work stays in this browser until you save a signature file.
- Signatures have alt text on every image, AA contrast in light and dark mode, text of at least 11 px, and motion that stops within 5 seconds.
- Every design fits a 343 px phone in 1, 2 and 3 columns.
